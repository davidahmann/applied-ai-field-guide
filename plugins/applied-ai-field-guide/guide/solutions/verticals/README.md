# Vertical Solution Profiles

Vertical profiles adapt the reusable business flows to an industry's objects, evidence, authority, risk, user surface, and operating measures. They are worked design profiles, not legal interpretations, policy engines, trained models, integration packages, or deployable products.

| Profile | Primary operator | Flow composition | Deliberately retained human authority |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Healthcare access coordination](healthcare-access-coordination.md) | Referral, authorization, or access coordinator | Request to activation + exception to resolution | Clinical, coverage, and patient-care decisions |
| [Financial-services investigation](financial-services-investigation.md) | Authorized investigator or operations analyst | Signal to investigation + exception to resolution | Case disposition, filing, customer treatment, and account action |
| [Industrial operations response](industrial-operations-response.md) | Maintenance, reliability, or supply operator | Risk to prioritized action + exception to resolution | Equipment control, safety decisions, work authorization, and production changes |

Apply a profile only after observing the target workflow. Replace every illustrative object, state, policy, metric, role, and interface with customer-owned evidence. Where the industry or jurisdiction changes a legal or safety obligation, the target organization's authorized experts and policies control.
