# Code of Conduct

## Standard

Be direct, technical, and respectful. Critique claims, controls, code, and evidence—not people. Welcome well-supported corrections, disclose conflicts of interest, and protect private or security-sensitive information.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualized conduct, deliberate disruption, and sustained personal attacks are not tolerated.

## Scope

This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, releases, and project-related public interactions.

## Reporting and enforcement

Do not open a public issue about a conduct incident. Contact the maintainer by [LinkedIn direct message](https://www.linkedin.com/in/dahmann/). If a report concerns the maintainer, use [GitHub's abuse-reporting process](https://support.github.com/contact/report-abuse).

Reports will be reviewed as confidentially as practical. Responses may include a private warning, content removal, temporary restriction, permanent ban, or referral to the hosting platform. Retaliation against a reporter is prohibited.
